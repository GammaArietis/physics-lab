The separate Maxwell campaign evaluates `F^2`, `F tilde(F)`, the Poynting
direction and electric/magnetic energy fractions. A null plane wave gives
orthogonal `E`, `B`, propagation direction and equal electric/magnetic energy.
Magnetic closed lines follow `div B = 0`; they are not discrete physical
stecche, and electric continuity is not a separate quantisation law.

A raw area/volume ratio is support-dependent and not Lorentz invariant. In the
registered scan the electric-to-magnetic energy ratios are determined by a
continuous amplitude ratio; no invariant ratio is selected by Maxwell's
action. Therefore `1/137` can be reproduced numerically only by choosing a
parameter, not derived by this geometry. A genuine derivation would need a
gauge- and Lorentz-invariant action/topological mechanism that fixes a
dimensionless coefficient and then passes independent QED observables.
