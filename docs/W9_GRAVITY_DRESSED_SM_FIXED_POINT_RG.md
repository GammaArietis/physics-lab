# W9 — Gravity-dressed Standard Model fixed-point campaign

W9 expands the W8 gravity control to the complete gauge group of the reduced
Standard Model and adds explicit matter-sector controls. The run is broad but
keeps a strict distinction between a literature-shaped term and an actually
derived common quantum-gravity truncation.

## Equations

For the three gauge couplings, the campaign uses

\[
 \beta_{g_i}=\frac{b_i g_i^3}{16\pi^2}-f_g g_i,
 \qquad
 (b_1,b_2,b_3)=\left(\frac{41}{10},-\frac{19}{6},-7\right),
\]

with GUT-normalized \(g_1\). For \(f_g>0\), the non-Abelian roots remain
Gaussian in this one-loop control, while the Abelian sector has the algebraic
root

\[
 g_1^{*2}=\frac{16\pi^2 f_g}{41/10}.
\]

The top-Yukawa and Higgs-quartic controls are

\[
 \beta_{y_t}=\beta_{y_t}^{SM}-f_y y_t,
 \qquad
 \beta_\lambda=\beta_\lambda^{SM}-f_\lambda\lambda.
\]

The \(f_y\) and \(f_\lambda\) coefficients are scanned as independent
minimal closures. They are not silently identified with a single published
gravity fixed point.

## Campaign

- 6 values of \(f_g\);
- 4 values of \(f_y\);
- 4 values of \(f_\lambda\);
- 96 coefficient slices;
- all algebraic gauge/Yukawa/Higgs branches retained;
- 4 initial states and 384 trajectories;
- five logarithmic scale offsets per trajectory;
- residuals and numerical stability eigenvalues recorded;
- no experimental data, fitting, ranking or automatic selection.

## Results

The scan confirms three structural facts within its declared control:

1. a positive universal \(f_g\) does not create nonzero perturbative
   non-Abelian gauge roots;
2. it does create an Abelian algebraic root, but the root is not by itself a
   derivation of the observed low-energy coupling;
3. independent \(f_y\) and \(f_\lambda\) terms generate additional matter
   branches, but their existence is not evidence for a physical prediction
   until all three coefficients are derived from one common gravity–matter
   truncation.

No branch yields a parameter-free derivation of \(1/137\). The correct output
of W9 is therefore a map of the fixed-point algebra and of the missing
closure, not a selected candidate.

## Literature boundary

The gauge ansatz is anchored in published asymptotic-safety analyses. The
gravity contribution to gauge beta functions is commonly represented by a
linear term in the coupling, while the precise coefficient is determined by
the gravitational fixed point and truncation. Published work also studies
gravity-dressed Standard Model and Higgs sectors. The campaign intentionally
does not promote its scanned \(f_g,f_y,f_\lambda\) values to physical data.
