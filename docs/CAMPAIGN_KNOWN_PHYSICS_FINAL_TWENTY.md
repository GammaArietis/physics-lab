# Final twenty-candidate known-physics campaign

Status: executed on 14 November 2026; `20/20 PASS`. The checkpoint and
per-candidate reports are archived under
`docs/validation/campaign_reports/known-physics-final-twenty-baselines/`.
Human review remains pending.

## Purpose

This is the final planned baseline campaign for the current known-physics
coverage wave. It executes the fourteen already archived K1-K5 candidates
together with the six newly admitted K6-K7 candidates. The purpose is to
verify reproducibility, scope closure and failure isolation across one
portable campaign record.

The word “final” refers only to this planned registry wave. It does not mean
that the laboratory contains all physics, that any candidate has been
selected, or that a physical theory has been empirically validated.

## K6 candidates

- `ising-lattice`: exact finite one-dimensional periodic transfer matrix.
- `harmonic-lattice-phonons`: finite periodic harmonic-chain normal modes,
  including the translational zero mode.
- `ideal-bose-gas`: finite-mode Bose occupations with `mu < epsilon_min`.
- `ideal-fermi-gas`: finite-mode Fermi occupations.

No K6 candidate takes a thermodynamic limit, performs material calibration,
or supplies an uncertainty model for condensed-matter data.

## K7 candidates

- `frw-background`: flat Lambda=0 FRW background with constant equation of
  state and explicit Friedmann/continuity checks.
- `cosmological-perturbations`: one linear scalar mode on a power-law FRW
  background, limited to local mode coefficients.

No K7 candidate fits cosmological observations, selects a perturbation vacuum,
computes a power spectrum, or claims a complete cosmological matter inventory.

## Campaign controls

- 20 registered candidate records; each must remain unselected.
- Eight isolated workers, bounded per-candidate timeout and resumable
  checkpoint.
- Deduplication by canonical record digest.
- Failure isolation with no automatic retry-based promotion.
- No search, finite bounds, external data, empirical comparison, fitting,
  ranking or automatic selection.
- Human review remains required after execution.

The machine-readable records are the campaign manifest, activation record and
the completed activation/report files generated by the runner. Reports must
be repository-relative and preserve the exact candidate record paths.

The human-review procedure is recorded in
`docs/HUMAN_REVIEW_CHECKLIST_FINAL_TWENTY.md`.
