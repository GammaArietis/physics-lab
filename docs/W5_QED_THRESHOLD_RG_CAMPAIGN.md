# W5 — Broad one-loop QED threshold RG campaign

W5 extends the W4 QED control with piecewise one-loop running. At each
registered threshold the coupling is matched continuously:

\[
\alpha(\mu_\mathrm{th}^{-})
=
\alpha(\mu_\mathrm{th}^{+}).
\]

Inside each regime the beta function remains

\[
\frac{d\alpha}{d\log\mu}
=
\frac{2N_f}{3\pi}\alpha^2.
\]

## Campaign executed

- 5 threshold schedules, including the no-threshold W4 control;
- 8 mathematical initial values of alpha per schedule;
- 17 bounded logarithmic scale offsets per trajectory;
- 40 trajectories and 680 recorded points;
- no external particle masses or experimental target;
- no fitting, ranking or automatic selection.

The threshold locations are normalized mathematical schedules. They test the
matching and piecewise-flow machinery but are not claims about the physical
electron, muon or tau masses.

## Result interpretation

The positive one-loop QED beta function remains positive in every active
regime. Thresholds change the slope of the flow and produce no non-zero zero
of beta. The only fixed point retained is therefore the Gaussian point
\(\alpha=0\). This is a result of the chosen one-loop truncation, not a proof
that richer systems cannot possess an interacting fixed point.

The next gate is a coupled beta system with explicitly declared additional
couplings, or a separate run that imports physical threshold data. Neither
step may use alpha as a fitting target. The W3A \(F^4\) operators remain
inactive until their beta functions and Wilson matching are derived.
